Boracay

Boracay is a small island in the Visayas known worldwide for White Beach, its four-kilometer stretch of fine white sand and clear turquoise water. It is a top destination for swimming, island hopping, and watching the sunset.

Read more about Boracay →

Palawan's Underground River

Near Puerto Princesa, this UNESCO World Heritage Site features a navigable underground river that flows directly into the sea. Visitors paddle through a limestone cave filled with stalactites, stalagmites, and large chambers. It was also named one of the New 7 Wonders of Nature.

Read more about Underground River →

Chocolate Hills, Bohol

More than a thousand symmetrical, cone-shaped hills spread across Bohol's countryside. They are covered in grass that turns chocolate-brown in the dry season, giving the landmark its name. A viewing deck in Carmen lets visitors see them stretch to the horizon.

Read more about Chocolate Hills →

Mayon Volcano

Located in Albay, Mayon is famous for its symmetrical cone shape, often called the most perfectly formed volcano in the world. It is also the country's most active volcano, so visitors enjoy it mostly from a safe distance as a backdrop for photography.

Read more about Mayon Volcano →

Siargao

Known as the surfing capital of the Philippines, Siargao draws visitors to its famous Cloud 9 break, as well as its lagoons, island hopping tours, and laid-back island atmosphere.
