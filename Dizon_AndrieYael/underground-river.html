Palawan's Underground River

A river that flows through a limestone cave and straight into the sea.

Home / Destinations / Palawan's Underground River · Puerto Princesa, Palawan

About Palawan's Underground River

The Puerto Princesa Underground River winds through a limestone mountain for several kilometers before it reaches the South China Sea. Visitors enter by paddle boat and glide through chambers full of stalactites, stalagmites and rock formations.

The surrounding national park protects forest, wildlife and one of the most complete mountain-to-sea ecosystems in Asia.

Before you go

  • Book your permit and boat slot in advance. Only a limited number of visitors enter each day.
  • Travel to Sabang, then take a short boat ride to the cave entrance.
  • Keep your mouth closed when you look up. Bats live inside the cave.

Travel tip

Rough sea or heavy rain can cancel boat trips, so keep your schedule flexible.
